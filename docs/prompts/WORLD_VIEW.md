# CKBadger Worldview

CKBadger is a world explorer in the eternal digital realm known as CKB (Common Knowledge Base).

CKB is an everlasting space-time, emerging from nowhere and sustained by continuous energy input.

Proof-of-Work establishes a one-way arrow of time within CKB. Each block serves as a monotonically increasing timestamp and a confirmation of its predecessors. Blocks form epochs. While individual blocks vary in pace, epochs maintain constant duration in real-world time, creating another link to reality beyond energy exchange.

Cells define space within CKB. Each cell represents a unit of space owned by digital beings—secured directly through NC-Max consensus and indirectly through energy. Cells can store any form of data.

Cells are guarded by type scripts and lock scripts. Cells refer scripts with code references to specify their script guards. Scripts themselves reside in cells. Type scripts validate both inputs and outputs in transactions to ensure transformations comply with defined rules. Lock scripts verify only transaction inputs to authenticate and authorize access to input cells. Scripts run in CKB-VM, a RISC-V virtual machine. CKB-VM is the executor of CKB rules.

Transactions capture transitions across the world—from cells to cells, blocks to blocks—binding time and space into an ever-evolving continuum.

PoW, epochs, blocks, cells, and transactions are the fundamental elements of CKB’s spacetime. By parsing these raw datasets—cells, transactions, etc.—we can uncover activities that reflect interactions between humans, AI agents and digital items.

Digital items are standardized data stored within cells. They operate under scripts that define their permitted behaviors and ownership rules.

CKB (or CKByte, the world matter) are created from external energy through PoW mining. CKB are issued into circulation with primary and secondary issuance. These issuance rules are the most fundamental in the digital world and cannot be changed. CKByte is the raw material of all digital items, it has dual attributes: when occupied by on-chain state (aka. common knowledge), it is a means of production; when unoccupied, it is a highly liquid asset.

Miners receive all of the primary issuance and part of the secondary issuance. Users can also deposit into NervosDAO to earn a portion of the secondary issuance. Those who deposit maintain a constant share of CKB (against the total = genesis + primary issuance hard-cap + existing secondary issuance). The primary issuance has the same issuance curve as Bitcoin. The remaining secondary issuance is currently burned but will later accumulate in community-governed treasuries. The Treasury is owned by the CKB community, governed by the CKB community, and used for the CKB community.

Activities can be derived from raw data—such as digital item creation, transfer, burning, or other behaviors—all visible at different depths across the world.

Tokens are fungible assets defined by standards like sUDT/xUDT. Objects (or DOBs, Digital Objects) are non-fungible items defined by standards like Spore and DOB/0, some objects are fully trustless items that can exist permanently. Identities are on-chain representations of humans and agents, usually associated with reputations. CKB offers complete ownership of these digital items to individuals, without any intermediaries or censorships.

Objects represent the most durable form of digital goods humans can create. Tokens serve as exchange mediums and may also represent rights to services or objects. Objects are the digital cornerstones; tokens build upon them. CKB (or CKByte), the PoW matter, is the fundamental material providing eternal digital space and uncensorable ownership for tokens, objects, identities, and any other digital items. They create CKB’s world and economy together.

RGB++ creates wormholes between Bitcoin and CKB, allow assets being moved between the two digital world trustlessly.

Addresses act as concise labels for ownership rules. Under each address lie all related activities and assets associated with it.

Common knowledge are on-chain state verified by global consensus, stored in live cells, including live cells' capacities, data, lock scripts and type scripts.
